Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information submitted through the DAD Reports website and healthcare survey is handled.

Last updated: 20 July 2026

1. Who we are

DAD Reports is an initiative of D.A.D. CLINIK PRIVATE LIMITED, presented as part of the Doctors At Door healthcare ecosystem. Questions about this policy can be sent to dadclinik@gmail.com.

2. Information you provide

The early-access form may ask for your name, phone number, email address, and city. The healthcare survey may also ask for age, gender, city, mobile number, and answers about your healthcare experience.

3. How information is used

  • To manage early-access interest and product updates.
  • To understand healthcare needs and improve the DAD Reports experience.
  • To respond to questions or requests sent to us.
  • To support planning for the first phase of the product.

4. Medical information

The current website survey does not request a medical diagnosis. Do not submit confidential medical records or emergency information through the website forms.

5. Sharing and retention

Information should only be retained for the purposes described above and for as long as reasonably required for those purposes or applicable obligations. We do not describe or authorize the sale of submitted personal information through this policy.

6. Your choices

You may contact us to ask about information you submitted, request a correction, or request deletion where applicable. Email dadclinik@gmail.com with enough detail for us to identify your request.

7. External services

This website links to social platforms and other external websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as DAD Reports develops. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
