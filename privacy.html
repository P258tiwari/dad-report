Privacy policy

This policy explains how we handle information you send through the DAD Reports website contact form and healthcare survey.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. Who we are

DAD Reports is an app from D.A.D. CLINIK PRIVATE LIMITED, part of Doctors At Door. Send questions about this policy to support@doctorsatdoor.com.

2. Information you give us

The contact form asks for your name and phone number. It may also ask for your email address, city, and a message. The healthcare survey asks for your name, age, gender, city, mobile number, and answers about your healthcare experience.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your messages and requests.
  • To send product and app updates.
  • To understand healthcare needs and improve DAD Reports.

4. Medical information

The website survey does not ask for a medical diagnosis. Do not send medical records or emergency information through the website forms.

5. Sharing and keeping information

We keep information only for the purposes above, and only for as long as reasonably needed for those purposes or for legal obligations. This policy does not allow the sale of your personal information.

6. Your choices

You can contact us to ask about information you sent, correct it, or ask us to delete it where applicable. Email support@doctorsatdoor.com with enough detail for us to find your request. To delete a DAD Reports account, see Account deletion.

7. Other websites

This website links to social media and other websites. Their own privacy policies apply to them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as DAD Reports develops. We will publish the latest version on this page with its date.
